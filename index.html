About This Project

A Branching Nature Documentary

Spring Walk is a short interactive nature documentary shot entirely on a personal smartphone. Using a two-branch structure, the viewer decides how the afternoon unfolds — a quiet encounter with wildlife, or taking cover as the rain moves in.

All three videos are encoded at 1:1 square ratio (1080 × 1080, H.264) so they embed natively on any social media platform or mobile screen. The branching logic is built with plain HTML5 and Vanilla JavaScript — no frameworks, no plugins.

Reflection

This project explores branching narrative as a form of interactive mobile media. By constraining footage to the 1:1 square format and pairing it with JavaScript-driven choice logic, I aimed to create an experience that feels native to social media while carrying a simple but genuine narrative arc. The nature setting allowed for two outcomes that contrast in mood — stillness versus shelter — without requiring elaborate production.
