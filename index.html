Two Moods, One Park

The revised project works best when each branch feels like a different way of lingering in the same place, not a jump to a completely different story world.

Route A

Lakeside Pavilion

This branch keeps the walk close to open water, framed architecture, and the small waterfall garden. It feels brighter, more architectural, and a little more expansive.

  • Boardwalk and pavilion views
  • Willow fringe beside the lake
  • Bridge and waterfall textures

Route B

Willow Garden

This route slows down and stays closer to benches, hanging branches, flowers, and koi. It leans into calm observation instead of bigger landmarks.

  • Curving shaded walkway
  • Close-up plants and flower beds
  • Quiet fish pond ending

About This Cut

A Branching Portrait of an Urban Garden

The website has been rebuilt around the new footage rather than the previous spring-walk storyline. The updated videos are rooted in Dayu Park imagery: the entry arch, lakeside pavilion, willow edges, waterfall bridge, lotus leaves, and koi water.

The interaction stays intentionally simple: one opening sequence, one moment of choice, and two alternate continuations. The page styling now borrows its palette from the videos themselves, mixing dark jade, stone, dusk blue, and warm gold so the interface feels like an extension of the place on screen.

Course Context

CMNS2016 - Social and Interactive Media
Interactive website presentation rebuilt to suit the revised vertical video edit.

Video Structure

The opening now introduces Dayu Park through the gate, boardwalk, and lakeside approach. Route A turns toward pavilion views, willow edges, and the waterfall bridge. Route B stays with shaded paths, flowers, and the koi pond.

Footage Source

All three web videos are based on the updated smartphone footage supplied by the author. The page copy no longer references the previous blossom, bird, wildlife, or rainy-detour theme.

Audio Handling

The supplied final exports already contain their own music and subtitle timing. This website presentation keeps those embedded edits intact instead of rebuilding them as separate assets.

Reflection

The redesign works because the site now describes the footage that actually appears on screen. Reframing the project around a specific urban park makes the branching choice clearer: one route is more open, architectural, and waterside; the other is softer, shaded, and close to plant detail. The interface is quieter now, so the pacing comes from the videos rather than from mismatched copy.
